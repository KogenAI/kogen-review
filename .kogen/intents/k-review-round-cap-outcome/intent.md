# End red-forever external reviews at the round cap

Slug: k-review-round-cap-outcome
Model: not applicable — no new state: the change takes the existing repair-stop rule out of external review sessions, leaving the existing per-session round counter as their only bound; the outcome is checked end to end
Acceptance:
- .kogen/intents/k-review-round-cap-outcome/acceptance.feature
- features/steps/k-review-round-cap-outcome__steps.js

In an external review session (`review.NewExternal(exec, desc, maxRounds).Review`), a reviewer that keeps repairing without reaching a green, approved tree must run until the session's round cap and stop with the executor's round limit (`executor.RoundLimit(maxRounds)`, `candidate/executor_round_limit`). The reviewer's last edits stay in the checkout. This is the outcome kogen's spec states in `spec/features/claude-code-full-lineup.feature`, scenario "External round caps preserve the candidate and do not restart another role": expected `candidate/executor_round_limit`, review role ran 6 invocations. kogen supplies that cap: the claude-code review role's cap is 6, and kogen-build passes it to `NewExternal`.

The kogen-review commit that changed this outcome is `932c061` ("Stop nonconverging reviewer repairs", Intent k-review-repair-no-progress-stop). It is the only change since `e5005fa` that alters how a review ends after a red verification. It added a stop in `run.evaluate` (`review/independent.go`): `candidate/review_blocked` with a synthetic blocking `changes` record after three consecutive identical final failures or twenty repair rounds. The External session shares `run.evaluate`, so the stop fires at the third identical red verification, before the cap of 6. That gives the reported `candidate/review_blocked: repeated identical final failures after 3 repair rounds; current failing stage/items: acceptance`.

What changes: an external session (the `run` built by `External.session`, `external: true`) no longer applies either repair stop. It neither stops on the identical-failure streak nor on the twenty-round hard cap. It writes no synthetic `changes` record, sends no `candidate/review_blocked` for them and saves no synthetic-block checkpoint. Each unsuccessful round still goes back to the same reviewer session as targeted feedback, exactly as before 932c061. The caller still gets `Env.Repaired` once per repair round, and the session ends with `executor.RoundLimit(maxRounds)` when the next round would exceed the cap. Counting `RepairRounds` and the failure streak for observability (`Result.Repairs`, the review's `Account`) may stay.

Unchanged: the native reviewer (`review.Independent` Review and Resume) keeps both stops, their messages, checkpoints and resume behavior exactly as k-review-repair-no-progress-stop specified. Also unchanged: external verdict validation, custody checks, an external reviewer's own `changes` verdict (`candidate/review_blocked`), review in parts, `External.Resume` (refused), and the round cap's source (the caller of `NewExternal`). This Intent does not change kogen's spec.

## Decisions
- kogen-review is changed so that the spec's stated outcome holds. The spec is not changed. (source: request)
- Resolve the conflict with k-review-repair-no-progress-stop's decision "External review's shared evaluation must obey the bounds" by scoping both repair stops (three identical failures, twenty repair rounds) to the native reviewer. An external session is bounded only by its executor round cap. The cap is always finite there, and kogen's spec expects its outcome. (source: request)
