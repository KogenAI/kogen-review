Fix the request-count trap reported in kogen-review at public pin ae445802, in `review/independent.go` `converse`: after the working request target the budget override asks for `submit_review` even though required unread parts make the verdict invalid. This is a request-count threshold, not an elapsed-time finding.

Reuse the current reminder, progress tracking and bounded allowance. At and after the working request target, ask for the read while required material is unread; allow a progressing reviewer to finish required reads. Once the parts have been delivered, ask for a verdict. Replace the unconditional submit-only budget prompt and forced choice, including their reintroduction through context reset and resume. Do not raise the target, add a setting, or turn it into a wall-clock timeout.

Keep verdict validation, complete requirement evidence, explicit failure on no reading progress, existing cancellation and per-response protections. On a route with independent unread evidence unavailable, use its established evidence contract rather than guessing. Carry the right prompt through the existing context reset and resume path. No provider call is needed or authorized by this work.

- Keep the current 12-request working target and counting boundary; change the required next action, not accounting or elapsed time. The current branch begins after twelve counted requests. (evidence: review/independent.go) (source: request)
- Use the existing reminder's unread read_file versus submit_review selection and bounded list of unread paths for both forced tool choice and text fallback; refresh the budget guidance as delivery progresses rather than retaining a contradictory submit-only instruction. Early submission with unread required material remains invalid; the target never authorizes bypassing these checks. (evidence: review/external.go; contract/external_test.go) (source: request)
- Context reset and resume keep cumulative request accounting, read-delivery state and allowance state, while carrying guidance appropriate to remaining required material.
